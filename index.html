Fish Rescue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Fish Rescue (“the game”) is made by Bulgasarinim (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You can play the whole game without giving us any personal information.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the game.
  • The only data that leaves your device is what the Daily Challenge worldwide leaderboard needs: a random anonymous player ID, your daily scores, and the first three letters of the nickname you choose (if you choose one).
  • Your full nickname never leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, pearls, unlocked cosmetics, stage results, daily challenge history and your full leaderboard nickname are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

What is sent, and why

The Daily Challenge has a worldwide leaderboard. To run it, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies), which processes the data below on our behalf:

DataWhyWho can see it
An anonymous player ID, created automatically the first time the game starts. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account. To keep your scores together and show your own rank. Us and Unity, as our service provider.
Your Daily Challenge scores, and the day each was played. To rank players on that day’s leaderboard. Everyone who opens the leaderboard.
The first three letters of your leaderboard nickname, if you set one. To label your score. Other players see it masked, for example “Fis****”. Everyone who opens the leaderboard.

Like any online service, Unity’s servers also receive the technical information needed to deliver it, such as your IP address, device and operating-system type and game version. This is used to provide and secure the service, not to identify or track you.

When the game starts it signs in to Unity anonymously, so the leaderboard is ready when you open it. Apart from that, nothing is sent while you play other modes, and nothing at all if you play offline; the leaderboard simply shows that it can’t be reached.

We do not sell your data, do not share it with advertisers, and do not use it for anything other than running the leaderboard.

Choosing a nickname

You are asked for a nickname the first time you play the Daily Challenge. It is optional — choose “Not now” and your scores appear as “???****”. You can change it at any time in Settings or from the leaderboard. Please don’t use your real name or any other personal information.

How long it is kept

Each day’s leaderboard resets at 00:00 UTC. Past days’ results are kept in Unity’s archive so that results are final and can be checked. Your anonymous player ID is kept as long as you keep playing with it; if you uninstall the game or clear its data, a new ID is created next time, and the old one is no longer linked to your device.

Security

All data sent to Unity Gaming Services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices and rights

  • Don’t take part: simply don’t play the Daily Challenge, or play offline. No scores or nickname letters are sent unless you play it.
  • Delete your leaderboard data: email us at bulgasarinim@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and the nickname letters and day(s) of your scores, and we will remove them. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may also have rights to access or correct your data; contact us at the same address.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The leaderboard uses only an anonymous ID and three masked nickname letters, and asks players not to enter personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has entered personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Service provider

Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies. Unity’s privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same address. Significant changes will also be noted in the game’s store listing.
